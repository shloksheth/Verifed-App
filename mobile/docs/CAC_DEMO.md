# Three menus for the video

Home's **Start with these menus** row opens:

- Fresh Kitchen Waterford Lakes: 35 imported food entries, all with local photos.
- Hawkers Orlando: 49 imported entries, all with local photos or illustrations.
- Taco Bell: 116 national menu entries, all with local photos or illustrations. The three listed locations share these product images.

For a clear dietary demonstration, select **Milk** in You and open Fresh Kitchen. Grilled Rosemary Chicken has a published dairy-free claim and appears as a match. Parmesan Cheese and Feta Cheese have declared milk and appear as conflicts. Compare Full menu with For my diet, open ingredient/source details, save a dish, then add a meal to the journal. Browse Hawkers for the local restaurant example and Taco Bell for the separate chain-menu layout and location selection.

The image scope is complete for these three imported menus, not the whole catalog. Source menus may differ from live branch availability. Photo coverage does not mean complete allergy information: Hawkers does not publish full ingredient lists for these records, and Taco Bell's imported ingredient phrases are not complete sub-ingredient declarations. Missing allergen evidence remains unconfirmed. Do not describe every item as allergy-safe in the video.
